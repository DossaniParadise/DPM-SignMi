How to paste it into Outlook
  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Outlook and click the Search box at the very top of the window.
  3. Type signature and click the Signatures result. (In some versions it appears under "Actions" or inside Settings — it's the same thing.)

Yours may look a little different — the steps are the same.

  1. Click + Add signature (in some versions the button says New). Give it a name — your first name is fine.
  1. Click inside the big empty box, press Ctrl+V to paste, tick both "Set default" boxes, then click Save (or OK).

That's it — every new email and reply now ends with your signature.
